Enter your personal details:

- Last name
- First name
- Email address
- Password (choose your own password)
- Confirm password

Then select the checkboxes for the **Terms of Use** and the **Privacy Policy**. Both are required.


Tap **Continue**.

<Aside type="note">
If an administrator has invited you, enter only these personal details. The "Company details" step does not apply. The app reports that your account has been created successfully. Continue with [Step 4: Logging in](/en/guide/user/04-einloggen). Steps 5 to 7 are reserved for administrators.
</Aside>
