<Aside type="note">
This step can only be carried out by administrators.
</Aside>

Navigate to **Settings → Feeders** and tap the **+** to the right of **Feeders**.

Go to the TouchTerminal of the Alma Pro that you want to add and open the following menu there. The numbers in the image correspond to the order of the steps:

<Image src={AufrufSetupCloud} alt="Menu on the TouchTerminal with steps 1 to 5" width={600} />

1. On the left, tap **Settings** (gear icon).
1. In the bottom bar, tap the **Touch settings** icon.
1. Tap **Accessories**.
1. Tap **Urban Cloud Service**.
1. Tap **Open settings**.

The TouchTerminal shows the **Overview Urban Cloud**. The red indicator next to **Registration at server** shows that the terminal is not registered yet. Tap the gear icon next to this line.

<Image src={Registrierung} alt="Overview Urban Cloud with a red indicator next to Registration at server" width={450} />

The TouchTerminal shows the **Urban Cloud registration** with a QR code. Scan it with the app.

<Image src={RegistrierungQRCode} alt="Urban Cloud registration with QR code on the TouchTerminal" width={450} />

The app reports **Feeder added**.


<Aside type="caution">
Synchronization can take a few minutes. Tap the refresh icon (↻) and, if necessary, log in again after 5&nbsp;minutes.
</Aside>

Back in the app, you have two options:

- **More feeders to add:** Repeat this step for each additional feeder.
- **All feeders added:** Continue with [Step 6: Licensing feeders](/en/guide/user/06-automaten-lizenzieren).
