<Aside type="note">
This step can only be carried out by administrators. A feeder only displays data once it has been licensed.
</Aside>

Navigate to **Settings → Subscription**.


Tap **"Upgrade my plan"**. There, select the feeders that you want to license. Feeders that are already licensed are greyed out and cannot be changed. The number of licensed feeders and the monthly costs update with every selection.

<Image src={Lizenzverwaltung} alt="License management with licensed, selected and available feeders" width={300} />

Confirm your selection with **Save** and complete the payment process. You are then redirected back to the app.



## Licensing more feeders later

You can add and license more feeders at any time. To do so, proceed as described above and select the new feeders in the license management.

Then continue with [Step 7: Creating barns and assigning feeders](/en/guide/user/07-stall-und-zuweisen).
