If you operate several feeders at different locations, you can create a barn for each location. Example: you distribute four feeders across two barns.

## Creating a barn and assigning feeders

Navigate to **Settings → Feeders** and tap the **+** to the right of **Barns**.

Enter a name for the barn. Under **Select feeders to assign**, use the box on the left to mark which feeders are located in this barn. Then tap **Create barn** at the bottom.

<Image src={StallAnlegen} alt="Creating a barn and adding available feeders" width={300} />

The app confirms with **All feeders successfully assigned**. Tap **OK**.

## Removing feeders from a barn

For an assigned feeder, tap the **×** and confirm the prompt with **Remove**. The feeder stays in your account and can be assigned to another barn.
