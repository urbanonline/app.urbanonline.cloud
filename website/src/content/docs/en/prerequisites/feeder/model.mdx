### Feeder Model Requirements

The Urban Feed Monitor App requires a feeder of the type **Alma Pro**.

### Identifying the Feeder Model

The easiest way is to tell the models apart visually by their housing and terminal:

<figure>
  <Image src={AlmaVergleich} alt="Comparison of Alma (predecessor) and Alma Pro" />
  <figcaption class="text-sm mt-2">
    Figure 1: Identifying features of the Alma (left) and the Alma Pro (right)
  </figcaption>
</figure>

| Feature    | <span class="p-0.75 bg-red-600 text-white">Alma (predecessor)</span> | <span class="p-0.75 bg-green-600 text-white">Alma Pro</span>|
|------------|-------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Housing    | Stainless steel, marbled | Stainless steel, brushed              |
| Lettering  | “KälberMAMA Alma”       | “ALMA PRO”                             |
| Terminal   | No separate terminal    | Touch terminal on swivel arm / separate |

### Next Steps

* If you are using an **Alma** or another predecessor (U40, U20), the Urban Feed Monitor App cannot be used. Contact your Urban sales or service partner.

* If you are using an **Alma Pro**, next check the [touch terminal](/en/prerequisites/feeder/terminal).
