## Touch Terminal Requirements

A touch terminal of <span class="font-bold">generation 2</span> (sold from 2020) or higher is required for the installation and setup of the Urban Cloud.

## Determining the Generation

### Visual Identification

The easiest method is to tell the generations apart by their housing:

<figure>
<div class="border border-gray-300 rounded-xs p-4">
<Image src={TerminalGen} alt="Overview of touch terminal generations" title="Overview of touch terminal generations" />
</div>
<figcaption class="text-sm mt-2">
  Figure 1: Touch terminal generations — left not compatible, right compatible
</figcaption>
</figure>

| Generation | Identifying feature | Compatible |
|---|---|---|
| Gen 1 | USB port vertically centered on the upper edge, Pro-face lettering | ❌ |
| 8" terminal | Red housing | ❌ |
| Gen 2 | USB port with blue LED below it, flush with the bottom of the screen, Pro-face lettering | ✅ |
| Gen 3–4 | Green frame around the USB port, black frame around the screen, Schneider lettering | ✅ |

### Determining the Generation via Software

If the visual identification is not conclusive, you can read the generation directly on the terminal:

* Make sure you are on the feeder’s home screen. If needed click the top `Home` button <span class="text-2xl">➀</span> <Home width={20} class="inline ml-2 mb-0" /> in the navigation bar on the left side of the screen.
* On the home screen, click the `Help` button <span class="text-2xl">➁</span> <QuestionMark width={20} align="top" class="inline ml-2 mb-0" alt="Help menu icon" /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.
* The installed hardware generation <span class="text-2xl">➂</span> is now displayed to the left of the language selection button, next to “Hardware” {/* UI-CHECK */}.

  For firmware versions lower than 25-04-V08, the installed hardware is not displayed. Contact your Urban service partner to update the firmware.
</Aside>

## Next Steps

* If you are using an **incompatible terminal** (Gen 1 or 8"), contact your Urban sales and service partner about replacing the terminal.

* If you are using a compatible terminal (Gen 2 or higher), next check the [firmware version](/en/prerequisites/feeder/firmware).
